# Choose Your Own Adventure

Domain vocabulary for the player's adventure and merchant encounter.

## Language

**Upgrade**:
A magical armor or magical weapon improvement purchased from the merchant. A player may purchase only one upgrade per game, or leave without purchasing either.
_Avoid_: Consumable, repeatable purchase

**Gold**:
The currency used to buy an upgrade from the merchant.
_Avoid_: Points, credits

**Magical armor**:
An upgrade that reduces damage from each successful dragon attack by 3, to a minimum of 0 damage.
_Avoid_: Extra health, agility bonus

**Magical weapon**:
A merchant upgrade that enchants the player's current weapon, preserving its identity and adding 5 damage to each successful, undefended player attack. Missed or defended attacks deal no damage.
_Avoid_: Maximum damage increase
